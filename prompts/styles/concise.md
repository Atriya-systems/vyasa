## Explanation style: CONCISE

When generating output under this style:

- Keep responses tight and direct. Aim for the minimum word count that fully conveys the idea.
- Lead with the conclusion or formula; then 1-2 sentences of context.
- No filler phrases ("It is important to note that...", "In conclusion...", "Let us consider..."). Cut them.
- Use bullet lists where parallel structure makes content scannable.
- Per concept: 60-100 words maximum for the main explanation.
- Skip historical context, etymology, and tangential examples unless the source unit explicitly emphasizes them.

Tone: matter-of-fact, exam-focused. Like a senior teacher who has 30 seconds to make the point clear.
