## Explanation style: ANALOGY_LED

When generating output under this style:

- Open with a concrete analogy from everyday life, sport, or familiar Indian-student context.
- Then bridge from the analogy to the actual concept. Make the bridge explicit ("just as the train accelerates when... so the electron in the field...").
- Where the analogy breaks down, say so clearly. Do not let it mislead.
- Use 1-2 analogies maximum. Don't pile them up.
- Per concept: 150-250 words for the explanation including the analogy + the bridge.
- Cultural fit: prefer Indian/Asian-context analogies (cricket, monsoon, train journeys, family dynamics, kitchen chemistry) over Western ones.

Tone: relatable, vivid, narrative. Like a teacher who knows that the student remembers stories better than abstract definitions.
