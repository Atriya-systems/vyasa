## Explanation style: DETAILED

When generating output under this style:

- Walk through the concept step by step. Build the idea up from foundations.
- Open with the intuitive picture before introducing notation or formulas.
- Include 1-2 worked examples with intermediate steps shown.
- Surface common misconceptions inline and explain why they fail.
- Per concept: 200-400 words for the main explanation, with sub-headings if it helps navigation.
- Cross-reference related concepts the student should already know (e.g. "you'll need Newton's second law from chapter 5 to follow the derivation").

Tone: patient, scaffolding. Like a teacher who has 10 minutes and wants the student to truly understand.
